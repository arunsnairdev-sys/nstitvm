DINING RULES

Mess Guidelines

• Maintain silence and cleanliness in the dining hall.
• Be on time for meals — late entry may not be allowed.
• Do not waste food; take only what you can finish.
• Outside food is not permitted inside the mess without permission.
• Report any food quality issues to the warden immediately.
